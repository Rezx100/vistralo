## Why this exists

Vistralo's customers record screens, upload videos and screenshots, and capture other companies' websites. Any of those can contain someone else's copyrighted work: a design, a photograph, text, music playing in the background of a recording. This page says how a rights holder can ask for something to be taken down, and what happens next.

## What customers are responsible for

Customers must have the right to record, upload or capture what they bring to Vistralo, and to share what comes out. A Visual brief of a website is a review of that site. It describes how the page is built and includes screenshots as evidence; it does not give the customer any right to reuse the site's design, text, images or code.

## Where content can be seen

A project is private to the account that made it. The only public surface is a share link, which the customer creates and which shows one walkthrough or one brief to anyone who has the link until it expires or is revoked. A complaint will usually be about a share link, so include it.

## How to send a complaint

Email contact@vistralo.com with the subject "Copyright complaint" and include: `{{DESIGNATED_AGENT_NAME_AND_ADDRESS_IF_REGISTERED}}`

1. Your name, and the rights holder's name if you are acting for someone.
2. A way to reach you: an email address, and a postal address or phone number.
3. What the work is, with a link to the original if it is online.
4. The Vistralo share link, and where in the video or brief the work appears (a time, or a heading).
5. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the rights holder or by law.
6. A statement that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them. `{{WHETHER_A_STATEMENT_UNDER_PENALTY_OF_PERJURY_IS_REQUIRED}}`
7. Your signature, typed or handwritten.

Without the share link we usually cannot find the content, because projects are private and we do not browse them.

## What we do with it

1. We confirm we received it. `{{ACKNOWLEDGEMENT_TIME}}`
2. If the complaint is complete and appears valid, we revoke the share link or remove the content, and tell the customer what was removed and why, including a copy of the complaint. `{{WHETHER_THE_COMPLAINANT'S_CONTACT_DETAILS_ARE_PASSED_ON}}`
3. If it is incomplete, we ask for what is missing.
4. We keep a record of the complaint and what was done. `{{RECORD_RETENTION}}`

## If your content was removed and you disagree

Reply to the notice we sent you, or email contact@vistralo.com, and include: which content was removed, why you believe the removal was a mistake or that you have the right to use the work, your name and contact details, and a statement that what you say is accurate. `{{COUNTER_NOTICE_REQUIREMENTS_AND_JURISDICTION_STATEMENT}}`

We pass a complete reply to the person who complained. `{{RESTORATION_RULE_AND_WAITING_PERIOD}}`

This policy says what may not be recorded, uploaded, captured or shared with Vistralo. It is part of the service terms.

## Why Vistralo needs one

Vistralo records screens, opens public websites on your behalf, sends pictures of what it captured to AI providers, and makes links that anyone can open. Each of those can hurt someone else if it is used carelessly, so the rules below are specific to them.

## Recording and uploading

Do not record, upload or share:

- A person's face, voice, messages or personal details unless you have the right to. In some places recording a call or meeting needs everyone's consent.
- Passwords, API keys, recovery codes, card numbers, government ID numbers or similar secrets. A screen recording captures everything that is visible, including a password manager or a terminal. Check before you share, because blurring is not something Vistralo does for you.
- Health, financial or other sensitive information about someone else, unless you are allowed to process it and to send it to the AI providers you have connected.
- Content that is unlawful where you are or where it will be watched, including content that sexualises children, threatens or harasses a person, or infringes someone's copyright or trade mark.
- Malware, or instructions meant to help someone break into a system.

## Capturing websites

"Analyze a website" opens a public address in an automated browser, takes screenshots and reads the page's code. You may only point it at:

- Pages that are public. Do not use it on pages behind a sign-in, a paywall or an access restriction, and do not give it an address containing a username, password or session token.
- Sites you are allowed to study. A visual brief of someone else's site is for review and learning. It does not give you the right to reproduce their design, text, images or code.

Do not use it to copy a site wholesale, to gather personal data from pages, to get round a site's technical limits on automated access, or to load a site so often that it is affected. Vistralo refuses addresses that point at private networks and gives up when a site blocks automated access; do not try to work around either. `{{RATE_LIMITS_IF_ANY}}`

## AI narration and voices

- Do not use a voice that imitates a real person unless that person has agreed, or the voice was provided to you for that use.
- Do not present a narrated walkthrough as a recording of a real person speaking when it is not.
- Do not use narration to produce content that deceives people about who made it in a way that could harm them.

## Share links

A share link can be opened by anyone who has it. Do not use share links to distribute content this policy forbids, to send unsolicited bulk messages, or to host files for download that have nothing to do with a walkthrough.

## The service itself

Do not attempt to access another customer's projects, probe or scan Vistralo for weaknesses without permission, overload it, resell access to it, or get round a plan's limits by opening several accounts. If you have found a security problem, report it as the Security Overview describes; reports made that way in good faith are welcome. `{{SAFE_HARBOUR_WORDING_IF_OFFERED}}`
